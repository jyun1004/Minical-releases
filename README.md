<h1 align="center">Minical</h1>

<p align="center">Today’s date in your Mac’s menu bar. Click it for your month, week and day.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://github.com/jyun1004/Minical-releases/releases/latest/download/Minical.dmg"><b>Download for Mac</b></a> · <a href="https://jyun1004.github.io/Minical-releases/">Website</a> · <a href="https://github.com/jyun1004/Minical-releases/releases">Release notes</a></p>

<p align="center"><img src="https://jyun1004.github.io/Minical-releases/assets/minical.gif" width="380" alt="Minical's panel switching between month, week and day views and between themes"></p>

Free for macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple; it updates itself. The interface is in English; a Korean translation is on the way.

This repository holds Minical’s releases, its update feed and its website. To report a problem, [open an issue](https://github.com/jyun1004/Minical/issues).
